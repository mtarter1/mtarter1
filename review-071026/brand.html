Brand guide

CTS identity, colors, and type in one place.

A working reference for the team and partners when Compliance Testing Services appears in proposals, publications, signage, or marketing collateral.

Brand foundation

Clear, practical, and grounded in field work.

Use the full company name, Compliance Testing Services, on first mention. CTS is appropriate after the full name has been introduced or when space is limited.

The brand should feel dependable, plain-spoken, and regional: focused on fuel-site compliance, responsive support, and accurate documentation.

Preferred fonts and text style.

Voice and usage

How CTS should sound in shared materials.

Lead with the practical need

Connect services to site compliance, uptime, documentation, inspections, testing, repairs, and follow-through.

Use plain language

Write for owners, managers, and operators. Avoid inflated claims or jargon when a direct explanation is clearer.

Keep the regional focus

CTS supports fuel storage compliance across MA, RI, CT, NH, and VT. Use state-specific language when it matters.

Make documentation visible

Reference clear records, accurate reporting, and organized follow-up when describing CTS work.
