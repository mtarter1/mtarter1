Testing services for working fuel systems

The testing required at a facility depends on its state, tank and piping construction, monitoring method, equipment, permit conditions, and prior results. CTS helps identify the likely scope, coordinate the work, document the outcome, and explain the next step when follow-up is needed.

  • Annual UST equipment testing
  • Overfill-prevention testing
  • Release-detection, alarm, and sensor testing
  • Tank, line, and line-leak detector testing
  • Sump, spill bucket, and containment integrity testing
  • Cathodic-protection testing and documentation

A clearer path from schedule to follow-up

Testing is most useful when it is connected to the site’s records and operating priorities. CTS works with owners and managers to review known due dates, prepare the facility, complete applicable tests, and organize results so open items do not get lost.

If equipment does not pass, CTS can help define the likely repair, retesting, documentation, or coordination needed to move the site forward.
