What the visit helps cover

Monthly inspection programs help operators keep required routine compliance work from piling up in any state where they operate. CTS reviews visible site conditions, notes concerns, organizes findings, and helps connect inspection results to the next step when a repair, test, or documentation update is needed.

  • Routine site walk-throughs
  • Visible equipment condition notes
  • Release-detection and alarm observations
  • Spill, sump, and dispenser area checks
  • Inspection documentation
  • Issue handoff and follow-up guidance

Why the monthly cadence matters

A regular inspection schedule gives owners and site managers a clearer picture of what is changing at the facility. Because every state CTS serves requires monthly inspection activity, the monthly cadence gives teams a consistent record to reference when questions, repairs, or testing deadlines come up.
