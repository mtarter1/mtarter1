Frequently asked questions

Answers before you schedule.

Find quick answers about quotes, compliance, scheduling, the service process, and ongoing support from CTS.

Billing & Pricing Compliance & Documentation Service & Scheduling Project Process Company & Support
01

Billing & Pricing

What are your prices?

Pricing depends on the scope of work, site conditions, location, and any specific requirements for your project. Once we review the details, we'll provide a clear quote before scheduling.

What are your payment options and terms?

Payment options and terms will be reviewed during the quoting process. Any deposit, balance due, or payment timeline will be clearly outlined before work begins.

Do you offer free estimates or consultations?

Yes, we're happy to discuss your needs and provide guidance before getting started.

What information do you need to provide a quote?

Helpful information includes the site address, scope of work, preferred timeline, and any photos, plans, or documentation you may have.

02

Compliance & Documentation

What compliance requirements apply to my site?

Compliance requirements vary based on your site, location, and the type of work needed. Our team can help review what applies and guide you through the next steps.

What if my site does not meet compliance requirements?

If your site does not meet applicable requirements, we'll explain the issue and recommend the appropriate next steps.

Do you handle permitting or required documentation?

If permitting or documentation is required, we can help determine what is needed and guide you through the process.

Do you provide documentation after the work is completed?

Yes, when applicable, we can provide documentation outlining the work completed and any relevant compliance or project details.

03

Service & Scheduling

How far in advance will you schedule?

We recommend reaching out as early as possible to secure your preferred date. Availability may vary depending on the season, project size, and location.

What areas do you service?

We service a range of locations and can confirm availability once we know your site address.

Can you accommodate rush or emergency requests?

We do our best to accommodate urgent requests whenever our schedule allows. Contact us directly and we'll let you know what options are available.

Will work disrupt normal business operations?

We work to complete projects as efficiently as possible while minimizing disruption. If your site has specific hours or access requirements, we'll coordinate around them when possible.

04

Project Process

What happens after I request a quote?

After you submit a request, our team will review the details and follow up if additional information is needed. From there, we'll provide pricing, scheduling options, and next steps.

How long does the process usually take?

Timelines depend on the scope of work, scheduling availability, and any site-specific requirements. We'll provide an estimated timeline once we understand your project.

What should I do to prepare my site before service begins?

Preparation may vary depending on the work being completed. Before your scheduled service, we'll let you know if anything needs to be cleared, accessed, or made available.

05

Company & Support

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we are licensed and insured. Documentation can be provided upon request.

Can you work with property managers, contractors, or corporate teams?

Yes, we regularly coordinate with property managers, contractors, business owners, and corporate teams to keep projects organized and on schedule.

Do you offer ongoing service, maintenance, or recurring inspections?

Yes, ongoing service or recurring inspections may be available depending on your needs. Contact us to discuss the best option for your site.

Who should I contact if I have questions after the job is complete?

You can contact our team directly with any follow-up questions. We're happy to review completed work, documentation, or next steps with you.
