About CTS

A trusted partner for safe, compliant operations.

Compliance Testing Services helps businesses and organizations navigate environmental requirements, maintain critical site systems, and resolve issues before they interrupt operations.

Built around practical
fuel-site compliance.

Founded in 2000 by Shawn P. Conrad, CTS was built around a simple but important purpose: helping fuel site owners and operators stay compliant, prepared, and confident in the condition of their systems.

For more than 25 years, CTS has supported independent owners, growing teams, municipal departments, gas stations, and commercial facilities with reliable testing, inspection, and compliance services. What began as a specialized service provider has grown into a trusted partner for customers who need accurate work, clear documentation, and responsive support year after year.

At CTS, we understand that compliance is rarely a one-time need. Requirements change, deadlines come up quickly, equipment ages, and small issues can become larger problems if they are not handled properly. That is why we focus on making the process practical and organized for every customer we serve.

From the first call to the final report, our goal is to provide dependable service, accurate testing, and the guidance customers need to keep their sites moving forward.

Getting Started

From the first call to lasting peace of mind.

CTS does not just handle one-off testing appointments. We help customers manage site compliance from beginning to end, whether they operate a single location or oversee multiple facilities.

When a customer contacts CTS, our team works to understand the site, identify the services needed, coordinate scheduling, complete the required testing or inspection, and provide clear documentation for compliance records.

This approach helps customers stay ahead of deadlines, avoid unnecessary confusion, and keep better control over their compliance responsibilities.
